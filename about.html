 2019 — The Beginning
One warehouse, three engineers, zero visibility.
Co-founders Ravi, Meena, and Sanjay were ops engineers at India's largest third-party logistics provider. Every day they watched shipments vanish into black holes — missed handoffs, broken integrations, hours spent on the phone chasing drivers.

They quit on a Friday. By Monday they were building Stackly.

Our Story

From a garage in
Koramangala to
180+ countries.

We started with one simple premise: if airlines can track every bag in real time, why can't logistics companies track every package? The answer, we quickly discovered, wasn't technology — it was fragmentation.

"We didn't build a tracking tool. We built the connective tissue that the logistics industry was missing."
— Ravi Shankar, CEO & Co-founder

In our first year, we integrated with 12 carriers and onboarded 50 paying customers — all through cold emails and product demos at logistics conferences. By 2021, we were processing 100,000 shipments a day and had closed our Series A.

Today, Stackly is the intelligence layer that connects warehouses, carriers, drivers, and customers in a single unified feed. We're not a tracking company. We're a data company that happens to move packages.
